# Japanese

Beginning to learn Japanese is no mere task. Knowing only of French, English and some Chinese leaned in high school, Chinese helps for kanji, but I still got to learn the kana alphabets : hiragana and katakana.

For know, I am using a PDF of Minna no nihongo Shokyu (volume I, 2nd Edition), so I'll try this manual. It seems a bit steep, so I also got a PDF of the minna-no-nihongo-I tanslation and grammatical notes on hand.

It's an image PDF so I can't copy and paste to help.

The manual "Premier cahier des Kana" from the French INALCO Japan Department (2014) found online help with roman ji prononciation.  

My father lend me the manual (japanese-for-busy-people volume I)[https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/671267/japanese-for-busy-people-i-by-ajalt/] 
so I will learn from it the first lessons. 

It is the version where you don't learn the hiragana, katakana or kanji first : you learn with the romaji translitteration first. 

I don't like this idea much as I tend to do some easy prononciation mistakes because of it. Still, learning the basis without the burden of the writting system is interesting as it allows to rapidly undersand a dialogue.

# First lesson

こんにちは Konnichiwa :  hello bonjour 

kon : jour 
i : particule du sujet du propos 
wa : particule de la cible target. Voir lesson spécifique. katakana ha prononcé wa dans cette situation. Utilier wo ou ha pour le clavier wozc

こんにちわ　今一話 


## XはYです

X i Y desu  X au sujet de Y est/être. 

僕はあサチャです。
bokuwa sacha desu

boku = watashi in more masculine version  je suis / about me 

 です desu : to be, is. 

バナナは甘いです。

今日は土曜日です。
Kyō wa doyōbi desu.
Aujourd’hui, c’est samedi.
Today is Saterday
